# Villager model licence

- Model: **Animated Human** by **Quaternius**
  (https://opengameart.org/content/animated-human-low-poly; also on quaternius.com).
- Licence: **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain dedication; the pack's own License.txt),
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- The pack ships FBX, DAE, OBJ and .blend, no glTF. `villager.glb` was exported from the
  pack's `Animated Human.blend` with Blender 5.0.1 (as the `bpy` Python module): the
  glTF exporter with every action (Death, Idle, Jump, Punch, Run, Walk, Working), Y up. Two
  empty helper actions were dropped. Nothing else changed.
- `villager_<skin>_<n>.png` are the pack's six *Clothed* palette textures, copied as they are
  (`ClothedLightSkin*.png`, `ClothedDarkSkin*.png`). The glTF doesn't embed a texture; the
  game applies one palette per villager.
